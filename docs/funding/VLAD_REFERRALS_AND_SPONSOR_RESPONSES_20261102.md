## Permanent MedScale mission and truthful status

**Mission:** build a free, open-source, clinician-controlled, on-device alternative to commercial ambient clinical documentation systems such as Abridge, accessible to health professionals without mandatory cloud subscriptions or vendor lock-in. Privacy and evidence-linking are central design priorities. The *current* Rust/Tauri engineering beta supports opt-in local Whisper transcription of licensed nonpatient audio, bounded FHIR R4 interoperability, encrypted local workspaces, provenance and synthetic QA. It is **not** a validated clinical scribe, not approved for real patient data, and not a production deployment. Every sponsor-facing message must distinguish current capabilities from roadmap (ambient clinical encounter transcription, reviewed SOAP notes, source citations, Arabic/English clinical evaluation, EHR integration).
