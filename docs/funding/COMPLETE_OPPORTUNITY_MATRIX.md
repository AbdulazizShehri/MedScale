**Last consolidated: 2026-11-02. Research and application planning, not evidence of submission, selection or award.**

## Applicant profile used throughout

MedScale is self-funded, independently maintained, Apache-2.0 licensed, publicly available as source code, with official website https://medscale.si/ and GitHub https://github.com/AbdulazizShehri/MedScale . Its founder is a **University of Melbourne MPH graduate** who still has a functioning student-domain email. **They are not currently enrolled as a student.** There is no verified current University research appointment, MedScale incorporation, institutional sponsorship or third-party funding. Do not imply otherwise.

MedScale is a nonclinical engineering Beta. No real patient record processing is authorized, and research workloads must use synthetic health fixtures and licensed public nonpatient data. The project's university graduate credential may truthfully be mentioned as background, but cannot substitute for an ongoing student or academic appointment.

## Application statuses defined

- **APPLY_NOW:** Public programme aligns with currently declared applicant identity; funder still decides whether to accept.
- **VERIFY_FIRST:** Potentially useful, but one or more eligibility or availability conditions remain unverified.
- **FUTURE_CONDITIONAL:** Preserve as an opportunity if actual enrollment, research appointment, company registration, investor sponsorship or community adoption later changes.
- **NOT_NOW:** Currently ineligible, closed, or too early; do not claim applicant eligibility or submit a misleading application.
- **FOUNDER_WRITES:** Programme restricts AI-authored applications. Do not draft or submit AI-written answers.
