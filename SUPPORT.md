# Getting help with MedScale

MedScale is a community-developed **engineering preview for synthetic data only**, not a clinical service or a commercial patient-support channel.

- **Questions and ideas:** use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/AbdulazizShehri/MedScale/discussions).
- **Reproducible bugs:** use the [bug-report template](https://github.com/AbdulazizShehri/MedScale/issues/new/choose) after searching existing Issues.
- **Feature proposals or documentation gaps:** use the appropriate [Issue template](https://github.com/AbdulazizShehri/MedScale/issues/new/choose).
- **Pull-request help and local development:** read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [desktop development](apps/desktop-tauri/README.md).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** privately report via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do **not** post exploit details in Discussions or Issues.

Maintainers are volunteers or independent contributors and may not be able to answer every request. No deployment, regulatory, medical or incident-response SLA is offered.

**Do not upload real patient records or identifying health information.** MedScale must not be used to make care decisions; seek qualified clinicians and appropriate healthcare services for medical questions.
