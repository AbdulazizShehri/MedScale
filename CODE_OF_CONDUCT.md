# MedScale Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a welcoming, constructive open-source community. Participation is open to people of every background and experience level. Contributors, maintainers and visitors are expected to treat one another respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous, inclusive, patient and open to constructive disagreement.
- Give technical feedback on ideas, code and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Explain decisions clearly, cite reproducible evidence where possible and help new contributors find their way.
- Respect privacy, credit original contributors and honor upstream licenses.
- Keep conversations relevant to the project and its documented, synthetic-only scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks or intimidation.
- Sexualized attention, stalking, unwelcome personal contact or publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately misleading claims, impersonation, spam or malicious contributions.
- Posting real patient information, private medical records, secrets or unredacted personal data in public project spaces.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, warn participants, restrict interaction, temporarily suspend involvement or permanently block repeated or serious violations. Decisions should be proportionate, documented as appropriate and focused on participant safety.

To report community misconduct, use GitHub's report/abuse mechanisms or contact repository maintainers through an appropriate private channel where available. **Do not post personal details or sensitive evidence publicly.** For technical security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, reviews and other official MedScale community spaces.
