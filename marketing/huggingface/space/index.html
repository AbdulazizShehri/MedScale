What makes MedScale different

Intelligence you can inspect. Tools you can own.

We believe trust isn't a promise on a website. It comes from visible sources, explicit boundaries, inspectable code, and decisions that remain in human hands.

01 / PRIVACY⌁

Keep control of your workspace.

The Rust Core and local AI packs run on hardware you control. No automatic workspace upload, product telemetry, or mandatory cloud account for local beta use.

02 / EVIDENCE⊞

See the evidence, not just an answer.

Inspect which records support a claim, where sources disagree, and when the system should abstain rather than guess.

03 / OPENNESS◇

Open work, accountable choices.

Explore the code, test evidence, qualification status, and boundaries around model admission and execution. No mystery behind the interface.
