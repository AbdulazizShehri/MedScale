# MedScale — Open Health AI

**Better health intelligence. Within everyone's reach.**

[Download MedScale Beta](https://medscale.si/download/) · [Source code](https://github.com/AbdulazizShehri/MedScale) · [Product guide](https://medscale.si/product/) · [Current qualification gates](https://github.com/AbdulazizShehri/MedScale/blob/main/docs/qualification/EXTERNAL_GATES.md)

MedScale is a free, open-source, local-first desktop workspace for exploring health records, evidence, research, models and accountable AI workflows. Its Rust Core keeps provenance, policy and audit decisions inspectable. Local beta use requires no MedScale cloud account or automatic workspace uploads.

This Space is an **informational, static product showcase**, not a model endpoint or clinical tool. See the separate [SafeOCR synthetic demo](https://huggingface.co/spaces/MedScaleAI/SafeOCR) and [SafeOCR-LabGold benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MedScaleAI/SafeOCR-LabGold) for inspectable research.

**Current safety and release scope:** Engineering beta for synthetic data only; no authorization for real patient records, diagnosis or live clinical deployment. Installers are unsigned. Catalogued third-party models are not necessarily MedScale-developed, installed, endorsed or independently qualified. Any user-enabled online service may transmit data under separate terms. [Read the privacy model](https://github.com/AbdulazizShehri/MedScale/blob/main/docs/security/TRUST_AND_PRIVACY_MODEL.md).

The MedScale design and writing are independent, not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenMed or Abridge.
