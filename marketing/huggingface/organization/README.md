## Health AI should be accessible, accountable, and under your control

MedScale is an independently developed **Apache-2.0, local-first health intelligence desktop project**. It brings research, evidence-linked records, AI models, audio workflows and governance together through a shared Rust Core. The Windows/macOS engineering beta runs on your computer without a mandatory MedScale cloud account or automatic workspace uploads.

| Privacy by design | Evidence first | Transparent model lifecycle | Open-source engineering |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Local workflows and encrypted workspace components | Record provenance, conflicts, gaps, and review state | Catalogued, admitted, running and qualified are different states | Rust Core, Tauri + React desktop, CLI, tests and public documentation |

## Our standards

**No invented clinical claims.** The product remains in a synthetic-data engineering beta. No model or system has been presented as clinically validated, and listed third-party models are not MedScale-original weights. We publish reproducible evidence and name limitations as clearly as capabilities.
